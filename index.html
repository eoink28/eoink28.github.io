02 Brain

Ideas I couldn't leave alone

Most of what I build starts as a question I keep turning over.

HERMES

An autonomous CubeSat concept with a ground station network to go with it. The goal is a satellite that detects, isolates and recovers from its own faults without waiting for help from the ground, while four tracking stations predict passes and hand the link over between each other. I'm developing it with mentorship from engineers at the European Space Agency.

The public repo is the mission control interface, which runs a simulated mission in your browser.

Mission control on GitHub

RoundReviewer

iOS app · computer vision

An iPhone app that logs every golf shot against the real shape of the hole, so after a round you can see where the strokes actually went. Drawing every hole by hand was never going to scale, so I built Theia: a service on Meta's open source SAM 2 model that splits a hole into fairway, rough, bunkers, water and green from one boundary you sketch. It runs on my own machine with no paid AI APIs.

RoundReviewer on GitHub

Poker Trainer

Side project

A No-Limit Hold'em trainer that grades every decision while you play. One coach checks your move against standard ranges and Monte Carlo equity. A second one reads the whole hand back and tells you which leak to fix first.

Poker Trainer on GitHub
